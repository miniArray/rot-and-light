# Pitch: Rot and Light

**One line:** you are the last fungal king. You take back a world that the
lamp-makers burned bright, one put-out light at a time.

## The world

The fungal kingdom grew in the dark under the old forest. The surface people
cleared the forest, then lit the land. Lamps, braziers and lighthouses burn
on every road. Light kills spores, so the kingdom died back.

The twist: the lamps burn oil pressed from your dead kin. The city's light
is made from your kingdom.

You wake in the last dark place, a cellar under a lamp-lit inn, with five
sprouts and a cap that still glows.

## The player

The Rot King: slow, heavy and old. He cannot fight much himself. His spore
cloud is his voice. Minions inside it obey, and minions outside it wither.

## The horde: you grow it from what you break

A broken thing becomes a new sprout. The material decides the kind.

| Kind | Grows from | Role | Silhouette |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Barkcaps | Wood: barrels, carts, doors | Sturdy fighters, carry loads | Broad, flat cap |
| Bonecaps | Bone: graves, cellars, carcasses | Fast biters, poor armour | Thin, tall, spiked cap |
| Rustcaps | Metal: gates, armour, lamps | Slow, armoured, safe in light for a short time | Squat, plated cap |

## The enemy: light

- **Lamps** are fixed zones with hard edges. Sprouts in the light burn.
- **Lantern guards** carry moving light. They break your formation.
- **Lighthouses** are the bosses of each region. Their beams sweep.

Putting out a light turns its zone dark. Rot spreads over the ground there,
and the change stays. The map shows your progress as dark land.

## Core loop

Break things → grow sprouts → put out lights → the rot spreads → reach the
next light.

## Pillars

1. **Dark is safe, light is danger.** The player reads this at a glance, always.
2. **The world is your army.** Every breakable thing is a choice about which
   minions you grow.
3. **Grim world, cute sprouts.** The setting is serious; the sprouts are not.

## First level: The Cellar

1. Wake with five sprouts. Learn to move and to send.
2. Break barrels and grow Barkcaps.
3. Put out a candle: the first dark zone appears.
4. A lantern guard comes down the stairs: the first moving light.
5. Put out the inn's hearth. The inn falls to rot and becomes your first hold.

## Visual rules (cel shading)

- Three tone bands for lighting, and ink outlines from a screen-space edge
  pass, so the cost stays the same for any horde size.
- Two colour worlds. The rot is cold: teal, violet and glowing caps. The
  enemy light is warm: amber and white.
- Light has a hard edge on the ground. The player sees exactly where it is safe.
- Each minion kind reads by silhouette and cap shape, not by colour alone.
- A sprout must read clearly at the normal camera distance with 30 or more on
  screen.

## Comparable games

Pikmin (you command small plant creatures). Dungeon Keeper (you play the
villain's side). The light-versus-rot territory rule is our own hook.

## Risks

- Pikmin is the closest match. The light rule, the evil side and the tone
  must set this game apart.
- Two systems (territory and growth) must both be clear while 30 or more
  minions move.
- Persistent rot across levels costs save data and art for each state.
